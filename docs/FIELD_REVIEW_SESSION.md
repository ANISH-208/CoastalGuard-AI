# CoastGuard AI — Field Review Session Sheet

Use this sheet only for a supervised prototype usability review. It is not a marine-safety evaluation and does not ask participants to make real fishing or navigation decisions using the app.

## Before the session

- [ ] Explain that CoastGuard AI is a student prototype and does not currently have confirmed live IMD/INCOIS integration.
- [ ] Explain that demo data is synthetic and must not be used for real-world decisions.
- [ ] Obtain informed consent for participation and note-taking. Participation is voluntary; allow the participant to stop at any time.
- [ ] Do not collect names, phone numbers, precise fishing locations, vessel identifiers, or other unnecessary personal information.
- [ ] Use a test device and synthetic records only. Do not ask a participant to act on an actual warning in the prototype.

## Task prompts

Ask the participant to try these tasks without coaching unless needed for accessibility:

1. Find the region and source for a displayed bulletin.
2. Identify the bulletin's issue time and validity end time.
3. Explain what the “Passed current checks” or “Not confirmed current” label means in their own words.
4. Find the original source text and official source link.
5. Simulate an unavailable API and explain what the dashboard says.
6. Identify what they would need to verify with an official source before relying on information.

## After the session

- [ ] Thank the participant and invite corrections to your notes.
- [ ] Separate observed behavior from your interpretation.
- [ ] Do not claim representative results from a small convenience sample.
- [ ] Fix any wording that could be interpreted as a safety guarantee before another review.
- [ ] Store notes securely and delete them when no longer needed.
- [ ] Record whether community review is complete only after the responsible project lead has reviewed evidence and limitations.

## Stop conditions

Stop the session if a participant is about to use a demo or unverified record to make a real-world decision, if the prototype presents synthetic information as official, or if a privacy/consent concern arises. Direct the participant to official marine information channels instead.
